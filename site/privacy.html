Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25 · Controller: Austin Taylor, doing business as AST Labs · Contact: support@clipspike.com

What ClipSpike does

ClipSpike for Windows records a Twitch channel's video and chat, creates local highlight clips, and optionally uploads those clips to a storage account you choose. There is no public Mac release. The app is sold and licensed through Polar, which acts as merchant of record. The license is $39, one-time.

Data the app processes

  • Twitch chat and video: read anonymously to detect reactions and produce clips. No Twitch account or password is used.
  • Clip files and sidecars: stored on your computer and, if you choose, in your Dropbox or Google Drive account.
  • OAuth credentials: Dropbox and Google Drive refresh tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on a Mac, and on Windows are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) for your user account. Non-secret settings are stored in a local config file in your user profile.
  • License data: a license key, activation instance, and last validation time, validated with Polar.
  • Diagnostics: local logs and crash reports, written to a folder on your computer. They are not sent to us or to anyone else automatically. Tokens and credentials are scrubbed.
  • Update checks (Windows): the installed Windows app checks a download server we operate (downloads.astlabs.dev) for new versions at startup, which reveals your IP address and app version to that server. The portable Windows version does not check for updates.

Data we do not collect

The app does not operate a clip server, does not receive your clips, and does not require your Twitch credentials. It does not post to TikTok or any social network.

Third parties

  • Twitch — channel video and chat, subject to Twitch's terms.
  • Streamlink — local stream resolution.
  • 7TV — public emote metadata and images.
  • Dropbox / Google Drive — only if you connect them; uploads go to your account.
  • Polar — merchant of record: checkout, payment, tax, refunds, and license validation.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the Windows download and update files.

Web dashboard

The web dashboard at clipspike.com/app is separate from the desktop apps and has its own privacy policy. It shows public Twitch clips and can include a consent banner, optional ads, affiliate links, analytics (only after you consent), and a waitlist form.

macOS waitlist

If you join the macOS waitlist, AST Labs uses your email address to contact you about macOS availability. The form sends your address to our form provider when configured. You can also send the request directly using your email app.

Retention and deletion

Local clips remain until you delete them or enable deletion after upload. Remote clips remain in your storage account until you delete them there. License and order records are retained by us and by Polar as required for tax, fraud prevention, and support. Uninstalling the app does not delete clips or remote files.

Your choices

You can use Local only mode, disconnect storage, clear local history, and disable diagnostics. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data. Contact us at support@clipspike.com.

Security

Credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain or encrypted for your Windows user account, and network calls use HTTPS. There is no public Mac release. The public Windows 0.1.6 build is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen shows an unknown-publisher warning on first run. No method is perfectly secure.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced on this page or in the app.
