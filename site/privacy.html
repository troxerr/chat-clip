What ClipSpike does

ClipSpike is a macOS application that records a Twitch channel's video and chat, creates local highlight clips, and optionally uploads those clips to a storage account you choose. The app is sold and licensed through Lemon Squeezy.

Data the app processes

  • Twitch chat and video: read anonymously to detect reactions and produce clips. No Twitch account or password is used.
  • Clip files and sidecars: stored on your Mac and, if you choose, in your Dropbox or Google Drive account.
  • OAuth credentials: Dropbox and Google Drive refresh tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. Non-secret settings are stored in a local config file with restricted permissions.
  • License data: a license key, activation instance, and last validation time, validated with Lemon Squeezy.
  • Diagnostics: local logs and, if you opt in, crash reports. Tokens and credentials are scrubbed.

Data we do not collect

The app does not operate a clip server, does not receive your clips, and does not require your Twitch credentials. It does not post to TikTok or any social network.

Third parties

  • Twitch — channel video and chat, subject to Twitch's terms.
  • Streamlink — local stream resolution.
  • 7TV — public emote metadata and images.
  • Dropbox / Google Drive — only if you connect them; uploads go to your account.
  • Lemon Squeezy — checkout, payment, tax, and license validation.
  • Crash reporting provider — [PROVIDER], only if enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local clips remain until you delete them or enable deletion after upload. Remote clips remain in your storage account until you delete them there. License records are retained as required for tax, fraud prevention, and support. Uninstalling the app does not delete clips or remote files.

Your choices

You can use Local only mode, disconnect storage, clear local history, and disable diagnostics. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data. Contact us at support@clipspike.example.

Security

Credentials are stored in Keychain, network calls use HTTPS, and the app is distributed with Developer ID signing and notarization. No method is perfectly secure.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Changes
