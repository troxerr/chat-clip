Triggers

Four ways a clip starts

No hotkeys to remember mid-fight and no scrubbing the VOD afterward.

Auto

Chat spike

The detector counts unique chatters in 5-second windows against a rolling baseline, so a small channel can still trigger and one huge moment doesn't raise the bar for the rest of the stream.

Raid

Incoming raid

A Twitch raid starts a clip on its own, as long as the cooldown has passed.

Command

!clip or !highlight

You or a moderator type it in chat. Nobody else can trigger it.

Manual

How it works

From trigger to a file you can post

  1. 00:30

    Capture

    The recorder keeps a rolling buffer, so each clip holds 15 seconds before the trigger and 15 after. The moment sits in the middle.

  2. 9:16

    Reframe

    Your full frame stays centered over a blurred, scaled copy of itself. Nothing is cropped off the sides.

  3. .mp4

    Deliver

    The vertical clip is kept on this PC or uploaded to your Dropbox or Google Drive, under a folder for the channel.

  4. .json

    Record

    Each clip gets a sidecar file with its trigger, score, reaction counts, top Twitch and 7TV emotes, and where it was stored.

Reliability

Built to survive a real stream

Dropouts

Reconnects on its own

If the stream drops, the recorder reconnects and still finishes any clip that was in progress.

Uploads

Nothing lost to a bad connection

A failed upload keeps the local clip and joins a queue that retries in the background, even after you restart the app.

Encoding

Keeps watching while it exports

Clips encode in the background with your PC's hardware encoder, so chat detection continues during an export.

Questions

Before you install

Does it need my Twitch password?

No. Chat is read anonymously. The app never logs into Twitch.

Can I use Dropbox and Google Drive at the same time?

One destination is active at a time, with an optional local copy. If you switch, pending uploads still go to the provider they were queued for.

What happens after the trial?

New captures pause until you activate a license. A capture that is already running is not interrupted, and your clips, queue and settings are kept.

What do I need to install?

Nothing, to browse clips. The browser app is at clipspike.com/app. To capture your own stream, install the Windows app (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit). There is no public Mac release.

Can I record any channel?

Only record channels you own or have permission to record, and follow Twitch's terms and the streamer's wishes.
